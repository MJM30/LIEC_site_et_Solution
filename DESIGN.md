## Brand & Style
The design system for LIEC (Lyon International Education Consulting) embodies institutional prestige, academic rigor, and human warmth. Designed for aspiring African scholars and their families embarking on higher education in France, the interface balances diplomatic authority with supportive, encouraging proximity.

The visual style blends **Contemporary Academic Editorial** with **High-Utility Functional SaaS**:
- **Institutional Trust:** Deep French-navy structures evoke reputable French universities, consulates, and international excellence.
- **Warmth & Guidance:** Cream editorial backdrops prevent sterile bureaucratic feelings, fostering a human-first mentorship atmosphere.
- **Clarity & Decisiveness:** High-contrast sunshine yellow accents provide unequivocal calls to action and milestone celebrations, while turquoise serves as the signifier of personal academic progression, prepa pathways, and validated visa stages.
- **Dual-Purpose Versatility:** The system shifts effortlessly between public editorial storytelling (program presentation, testimonials, country guides) and dense, high-efficiency portals (dossier tracking, document checklists, mentor appointment scheduling).

## Colors
The color architecture prioritizes legibility, cognitive comfort during long administrative sessions, and unambiguous status hierarchy:

- **Institutional Navy (`#153A70`):** Anchors headers, primary brand navigation, authoritative badges, and formal typography. Conveys stability, legality, and international standard.
- **Guidance Turquoise (`#21B7A8`):** Applied to progress bars, completed milestones, secondary actions, and mentor highlights. Evokes optimism, development, and the journey across borders.
- **Solar Action (`#FFC94A`):** The primary CTA driver. Reserved strictly for forward movement: "Déposer mon dossier", "Prendre rendez-vous", "Valider l'étape". Must pair with `#153A70` or `#2D3748` text for contrast compliance.
- **Warm Cream (`#FFF4E7`):** The canvas accent for feature showcases, interview tips, editorial guidance callouts, and conversational assistance blocks.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** Base canvas for document cards, input fields, and application status boards.
- **Charcoal Neutral (`#2D3748`):** The standard body copy baseline, delivering superior readability over pure black.
- **Soft Slate (`#E2E8F0`):** Structural divider lines, card strokes, and subtle inactive track backgrounds.

## Typography
`Plus Jakarta Sans` is chosen for its crisp geometric construction, high x-height, and warm humanistic terminals. It effortlessly bridges the gap between formal French academic credibility and a modern, welcoming digital experience.

- **Headlines & Display:** Set in tight-tracking weights (600 to 800) using Institutional Navy (`#153A70`). Conveys assurance, clear leadership, and authority.
- **Editorial Sub-headers:** Utilize lighter lead paragraphs in Charcoal Neutral (`#2D3748`) to guide candidates through complex procedural steps.
- **Application Forms & Dense Data:** Small body scales and label typography retain open apertures, maintaining immaculate legibility on low-bandwidth mobile devices or high-density admin tables.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a structured 12-column responsive grid for portal dashboards and a centered 8-column reading column for editorial orientation articles.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column structure with `margin-mobile: 1rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. Bottom sheets replace modals; tab bars support mobile thumbs.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout accommodating split document-preview panes and navigation rails.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column system, max container width of 1280px with fixed persistent navigation and multi-column application trackers.
- **Rhythm Rules:** All layout intervals follow an 8-point harmonic grid (4px, 8px, 16px, 24px, 40px). Input and card internal padding strictly uses `space-md` or `space-lg` to ensure comfortable touch targets and balanced visual pacing.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is handled through crisp structural layering rather than dramatic, heavy shadows. This reflects clarity, precision, and administrative rigor:

- **Surface Tiers:**
  - Base: Warm Cream (`#FFF4E7`) for landing and guidance backgrounds; Neutral 50 (`#F8FAFC`) for the back office workspace.
  - Elevated Surface: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for data cards, checklist containers, and interactive forms.
- **Outlines & Borders:** All elevated cards employ a delicate 1px border in `#E2E8F0` to maintain clean separation under varied display lighting.
- **Subtle Ambient Shadows:**
  - Low Elevation (Resting Cards, Document Tiles): `0 1px 3px rgba(21, 58, 112, 0.05), 0 1px 2px rgba(21, 58, 112, 0.03)`
  - Mid Elevation (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns): `0 8px 18px -4px rgba(21, 58, 112, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(21, 58, 112, 0.04)`
  - High Elevation (Application Review Modals, Notifications): `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(21, 58, 112, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(21, 58, 112, 0.04)`

## Shapes
A balanced medium roundedness (`level 2`, 0.5rem base) conveys contemporary digital maturity without appearing casual or childlike:
- **Buttons, Form Inputs, & Standard Badges:** 8px (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Cards & Document Upload Wells:** 16px (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`).
- **Feature Modules & Modal Dialogs:** 24px (`1.5rem` / `rounded-xl`).
- **Status Pills & Progress Counters:** Fully circular pill shapes for instant visual recognition.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Conversion / Key Steps):**
  - Background: Solar Yellow (`#FFC94A`), Text: Institutional Navy (`#153A70`), Weight: Bold (`700`).
  - Hover: Tone deepened to `#F0B732` with a subtle 2px upward shift. Focus: 3px outline in `#FFC94A` with a 2px white offset.
- **Secondary Action (Academic Exploration / Document Review):**
  - Background: Institutional Navy (`#153A70`), Text: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
  - Hover: `#1B498C`.
- **Tertiary & Support Action:**
  - Background: Transparent, Border: 1.5px solid `#21B7A8`, Text: `#21B7A8`.
  - Hover: Light turquoise tint fill (`rgba(33, 183, 168, 0.08)`).

### Badges & Status Indicators
- **En Cours (In Progress):** Turquoise background (`rgba(33, 183, 168, 0.12)`), text `#0E746A`, accompanied by a solid turquoise status dot.
- **Action Requise (Missing Document / Payment):** Solar Yellow background (`rgba(255, 201, 74, 0.2)`), text `#8D6400`.
- **Validé (Visa / Admission Approved):** Mint Green tint (`#E6F7F2`), text `#0B6E4F`.
- **Dossier Soumis:** Institutional Navy tint (`rgba(21, 58, 112, 0.08)`), text `#153A70`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base Style:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, text `#2D3748`.
- **Focus State:** Border color shifts to Institutional Navy (`#153A70`) with an ambient 3px halo in `rgba(21, 58, 112, 0.12)`.
- **Helper Labels:** High-contrast 12px uppercase label positioned directly above the input with a 4px gap.
- **Validation Messages:** Clear iconography paired with contextual instructions (e.g., "Passeport valide au moins 6 mois requis").

### Cards & Dossier Modules
- **Public Program Card:** Pure white background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, topped with an optional 4px accent line (Navy for degree programs, Turquoise for PREPA sessions).
- **Administrative Checklist Card:** Grouped checklist items separated by 1px slate borders, featuring embedded status chips, upload button triggers, and timestamp updates.

### Checkboxes & Progress Tracks
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Custom 18px containers. In checked state: filled with Institutional Navy (`#153A70`) featuring a crisp white vector tick.
- **Milestone Progress Stepper:** Horizontal connected bar with step nodes. Completed stages turn Guidance Turquoise (`#21B7A8`) with check icons; current stage is ringed with Solar Yellow (`#FFC94A`).
